The field (`src/components/site/Field.tsx`, about 230 lines). Blue theme since 2026-10-05 (David: blue instead of yellow; resting palette deep indigo to pale sky, links #8db8ff dark / #2a5dcc light, cool navy frame). On load the field grows out of the middle as one shape with a slowly wavy outline stepped in whole cells, each cell popping a little bright as the edge passes (`LOAD` 1600ms, `POP`). David rejected a scattered pixel dissolve, 2026-10-05. Cells of drifting colour from folded sine waves (the field slides slowly sideways and rolls through its palette over about a minute; David asked for visible idle motion, the first pass was too still), every cell one solid colour, fading in on load. Hovering or opening a project blends the whole field into that brand's palette (neighbouring hues at muted mid tones, warm grey for near black brands) over 900ms; a new request mid blend blends on from wherever the field is. The pointer lifts nearby cells slightly. The field is window sized and anchored right, so opening the article never moves the cells. No logos in the grid, no fronts, no queue, no banner.

The article (`Panel.tsx`) lies UNDER the field. Standing rule from David, 2026-10-04: content is always revealed by the grid, never slid or faded in. Opening, the field clears its cells from the left as a soft staircase, rows moving in bands of 2 to 5 that trail the front by up to 4 cells and land straight (`REVEAL` 900ms; David rejected spiky per-row raggedness), exposing the article; closing, the field covers it again; switching projects covers the article, swaps the content, then reveals (App `covering`). The article takes as much width as it needs (`--col`, up to 1100px, always leaving the field at least 320px; text capped at 620px, screens use the full column so their UI is not dwarfed by the text), and the strip of field that remains holds the open project's logo drawn in the same cells as the field (David: every pixel the same size, no subdivided cells). Cells are 12px (`CELL`, matched by `--cell` in CSS) so a logo gets about 24 by 20 cells; each logo is cropped to its own shape, solid marks get their gaps widened, thin marks get extra weight, and Reve's bars map one bar column per cell (`PITCH`). Edges stay soft: partly covered cells warm through the palette's bright tone before white, and a blurred copy (`GLOW`) brightens the field around the mark (David preferred this to crisp on/off cells, 2026-10-05). The logo is white in both themes (David). In light mode edges fade straight to white with no tone in between, and the glow deepens the field around the mark so the white reads (hidden `Marks` rasterised by `buildMask`), lit from its centre once the reveal lands, the field dimmed slightly around it. Switching projects never hides the logo: the new mark joins the old in a shared frame and the outlines blend as distance fields over `MORPH` 1100ms, so one shape flows into the next, staying in the strip while the article is covered and revealed (David, 2026-10-05). The open project's colour is also the accent for every link, the sidebar included (`brandAccent`, light on dark, deep on paper, grey for near black brands; colour transitions 700ms). While a project is open its colours own the field (hover previews only when nothing is open). The active name shows a small close mark; clicking it again or Escape closes; up and down arrows step through all nine. Under 1000px the field is a band on top and the article slides up full screen with a close button. The address follows (`/projects/<slug>/`).
